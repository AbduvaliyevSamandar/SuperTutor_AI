# SuperTutor AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated**: 2026-05-14

## What we collect

**Account data** (when you sign up):
- Email address
- Display name (optional)
- Encrypted password (Supabase Auth)

**Learning data**:
- Practice session timestamps and duration
- Quiz results and weak topics
- Saved vocabulary words
- XP, streak, gems, hearts
- Chat message history (for personalization)
- Achievement progress

**Anonymous diagnostics**:
- Crash logs (no PII)
- Aggregated usage (no PII)

## What we DO NOT collect

- We do not collect contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers
- We do not sell or share data with advertisers
- We do not show ads of any kind

## Third parties we use

- **Supabase** (postgres + auth) — data hosting, EU region
- **Groq / OpenAI / Google Gemini** — AI inference (text/voice transcripts)
- **Microsoft Edge-TTS** — voice synthesis
- **Render** — backend hosting

Audio recordings (microphone) and uploaded images are sent to the AI provider for transcription/analysis but are **never stored** on our servers.

## Your rights

- **Access**: see all your data in the app (Profile → Statistics)
- **Delete**: Profile → "Akkauntni o'chirish" deletes everything irreversibly
- **Export**: email us and we'll send your data in JSON within 30 days

## Children

App is suitable for ages 13+. Younger users should use with parent supervision.
